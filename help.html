🚀 Getting Started
What is Campus Gig Board? ▼
Campus Gig Board is a student-to-student micro task marketplace. Students can post small tasks (gigs) with a bounty starting at ₹50, and other students on the same campus can apply to complete them. It's built for same-day, on-campus work — no platform fees, no complexity.
How do I sign up? ▼
Click "Create Account" on the login page. Enter your name, college email, a password (min 6 characters), and optionally your skills. That's it — you can start posting or applying immediately.
Is it free to use? ▼
100% free. Campus Gig Board charges zero platform fees. The full bounty amount goes directly from the poster to the worker. We don't take any cut.
📌 Posting Gigs
How do I post a gig? ▼
From the dashboard, fill in the "Post a Task" form on the left: add a title, description, category, bounty amount, and deadline. Hit "Post Gig" and it goes live on the board instantly.
What's the minimum bounty? ▼
The minimum bounty is ₹50. We recommend setting a bounty that fairly reflects the effort involved — for example, ₹200 for a simple poster, ₹500 for debugging code.
How do I manage my posted gigs? ▼
Go to "My Gigs" in the navigation. You'll see all your posted gigs with their current status, applicant count, and quick links to view applicants or manage the gig detail.
How do I accept an applicant? ▼
Open the gig detail page or the Applicants page. Review each applicant's pitch, timeline, and portfolio link. Click "Accept this Applicant" — all others are automatically notified. The gig moves to In Progress.
📨 Applying to Gigs
How do I apply to a gig? ▼
From the dashboard or gig detail page, click "Apply for Gig". Fill in your pitch (why you're the right fit), an optional portfolio link, and your proposed timeline. Submit and you're in.
Can I apply to my own gig? ▼
No. You cannot apply to gigs you posted. This keeps the marketplace fair.
How do I track my applications? ▼
Visit "My Applications" in the navigation. You'll see every gig you've applied to, along with the current gig status and your application status (Pending / Accepted / Rejected).
Will I be notified if I'm accepted or rejected? ▼
Yes! You'll receive an in-app notification immediately when the poster makes a decision. Check the 🔔 bell icon in the navigation bar for updates.
💸 Payments & Bounties
How are payments made? ▼
Payments are made peer-to-peer via UPI. Once a gig is marked Completed, the poster pays the worker directly using their UPI QR code — found on the worker's profile page. No payment gateway, no delay.
Where do I add my UPI ID? ▼
Go to your Profile page → Edit Profile → enter your UPI ID (e.g. yourname@upi). A QR code is auto-generated and displayed on your profile so posters can scan and pay you instantly.
What if the poster doesn't pay after completion? ▼
We recommend agreeing on payment terms before starting. Since all users are verified campus students, most disputes can be resolved in person. Always screenshot your agreement and keep communication on record.

👤 Account & Profile
How do I edit my profile? ▼
Go to Profile → Edit Profile. You can update your display name, bio, skills (comma-separated), and UPI ID. Click Save Changes and it updates immediately in the database.
What is the Leaderboard? ▼
The Leaderboard ranks students across three categories: Top Earners (most ₹ earned), Most Completed (most gigs done as a worker), and Top Posters (most gigs posted). It resets as data changes live.
Can I change my password? ▼
Password change is coming in a future update. For now, contact the admin if you need a reset.

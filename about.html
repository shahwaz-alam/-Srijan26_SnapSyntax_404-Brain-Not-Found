📋 What Is This?

A hyper-local marketplace
built for campus life.

Campus Micro-Gig Board is a student-to-student task marketplace built specifically for college campuses. Unlike Fiverr or Upwork — which are bloated, expensive, and require KYC — our platform is lightweight, local, and instant.

Post a task with a small bounty (starting at ₹50), and let fellow students on your campus apply. You pick the best applicant, the work gets done, and the bounty gets paid. Simple as that.

Every gig goes through a clear Open → Applied → In Progress → Done lifecycle — so both parties always know where things stand.

  • 🎯
    Campus-local only
    Gigs are for your campus. No remote strangers — just people who understand the same deadline pressure you do.
  • 💸
    Zero platform fees
    We don't take a cut. Every rupee of the bounty goes directly to the student who did the work.
  • ⚡
    Same-day turnaround
    Built for quick tasks. Post a gig, get it done today. No month-long project timelines.
  • 🔒
    Student-verified
    Only students with college email accounts can post or apply — keeping the community trusted and accountable.
🤔 Why Does It Exist?

Because campus life has
a skills gap problem.

😩
Platforms are overkill
Fiverr, Upwork, Toptal — all built for professionals charging thousands. A ₹200 poster job doesn't belong there, and neither does the 30% platform fee.
📱
WhatsApp groups are chaos
Messages get buried in 200-person class chats. There's no structure, no accountability, no way to track who said yes and actually delivered.
🎨
Skills go unused
Every campus has students with Figma skills, Python skills, video editing chops — with zero outlet to monetise them outside of internships.
🤝
Peer economy is powerful
Students get it. A classmate knows what "send by 11 PM" means. Peer-to-peer gigs build trust, community, and real-world experience simultaneously.
⚙️ How It Works

Four steps. Done.

📌
STEP 01
Post a Gig
Add a title, description, category, deadline, and bounty amount. Takes 60 seconds.
🚀
STEP 02
Get Applications
Students browse the feed, apply with a pitch and portfolio link. Status moves to Applied.
✅
STEP 03
Accept & Work
Accept the best applicant. Gig status flips to In Progress. Work begins.
💸
STEP 04
Mark Done & Pay
Mark the gig Completed. Pay the bounty. Both profiles get updated.
